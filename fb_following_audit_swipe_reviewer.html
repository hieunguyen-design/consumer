Import Your Facebook Following Data

100% local in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.

How to export from Facebook (bypasses CAPTCHA scraping):

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
  2. Select Download your information → Some of your information → check Followers and following (and optionally Friends).
  3. Choose format JSON, download, and select the .json files below (e.g. following.json, followers.json, friends.json).
